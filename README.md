# Sunrise Creatures

**Creature mods for [Sunrise](https://playsunrise.net)**, the Rust reimplementation of Minecraft Java 26.3 (formerly
Horizon). A vulture to tame and fly, butterflies and moths, fireflies, earthworms for your garden, a one-eyed giant
shepherd, shy fairies, scorpions, spirits of living lightning, jellyfish, flying fish, seagulls, ladybugs, stick and leaf insects; and Minecraft's own creatures in the guide.

- **Install the ones you like**: each mod works alone, with one shared library.
- **Native mods**, written in Rust on Sunrise's mod API, with their own models, animations, sounds and behaviours.
- **In beta**: played and tested in single player; multiplayer and balance come later.

## Install

You need **the Sunrise launcher**, from [playsunrise.net](https://playsunrise.net), and a Minecraft: Java Edition
account.

1. In the launcher's **Discover** page, find the creatures you like: each is its own mod, and installing one brings
   the library, **Sunrise Creatures**, with it. Also at [playsunrise.net/mods](https://playsunrise.net/mods).
2. Press **Play**. These are native mods: the launcher builds them for your game the first time (a few minutes).

Each version is also a [release of this repository](https://github.com/EAnathos/sunrise-creatures/releases)
(`<mod>/v<version>`), built and attested by the registry's release workflow, and reviewed before it is listed.

## In the game

The idea is to bring Minecraft's world to life: creatures that belong where you meet them, some common, some rare,
each with its own way of living, its own interactions with you and with the world, and something it is good for. A
vulture to tame and fly, worms that help a garden grow, fairies that light your way, seagulls that steal your bread or
fish for you, a cyclops guarding its flock at a cave mouth.

**The creature guide** ties them together. Every player gets one the first time they join a world. Its **bestiary**
gives each creature you come across a page, its model turning in 3D, with the biomes you met it in and how rare it is
(common, uncommon, rare or very rare). The page fills in as you play: the kinds and colours you have seen, and the
facts you find out by watching it live (what it eats, what hunts it, how to befriend it, what it does when you come
too close). Its **Items** tab shows what each creature drops and what it is for. With the
[Vanilla Bestiary](mods/vanilla_bestiary/README.md), Minecraft's own creatures get their pages too, found out the same
way.

Each mod's page tells you how to find its creatures and what to do with them.

Single player only for now; multiplayer comes later for the whole series.

## Build from source

The mods build with the repository's own tools (`cargo creature`, Rust) and Sunrise's `cargo sunrise-mod`:
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) has the setup, the commands and the conventions, and
[docs/PUBLISHING.md](docs/PUBLISHING.md) how a version is released.
