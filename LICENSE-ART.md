The artwork of Sunrise Creatures belongs to its author, EAnathos. It is not covered by the licence of the code
(`LICENSE`): that licence lets anyone fork and change the code for noncommercial purposes, but gives no right to the
artwork.

## What the artwork is

Everything in this repository that is an image or a model, and what is made from them:

- the models: the Blockbench files (`mods/*/sources/models/*.bbmodel`), the model code generated from them
  (`src/model*.rs`), and the block and item models (`assets/*/models/`);
- the textures, the icons and every other image: PNG, WebP or JPEG files, wherever they are (`assets/`, `docs/`, the
  modules' `page/`), including the pictures of the models (`docs/models/`), the banner and the in-game pictures;
- the same artwork as it appears in the game, in the released mods (`.smod` files) and on their pages.

## What you may do

- **Play the mods** as their author publishes them (on Sunrise's registry, or built from this repository), and show
  them in play: screenshots, videos and streams of the game.
- **Show the artwork on noncommercial fan sites**, such as a fan wiki, a guide, a review or a forum post: pictures of
  the creatures and their items, taken from the game or from this repository, unchanged except for cropping and
  resizing, each credited to "EAnathos, Sunrise Creatures" with a link to
  <https://github.com/EAnathos/sunrise-creatures>. A site whose host shows ads (a free wiki host, say) still counts as
  noncommercial, as long as you neither sell the artwork nor charge for access to it.

## What you may not do

- Use the artwork, or anything made from it (a changed version, a redrawing that copies it), in a fork of this
  repository, in another mod, a resource pack, a modpack that changes it, or any other software or game. A fork must
  remove the artwork and bring its own.
- Use the artwork for any commercial purpose: sell it, sell something that shows it (merchandise, prints, a paid
  product), or use it to advertise a product or a service.
- Pass the artwork off as your own, or remove its credit.

For anything else, ask the author (an issue on <https://github.com/EAnathos/sunrise-creatures>).

## Sounds

The sounds of the mods (`assets/*/sounds/`) are not the author's artwork: they are cut from recordings on
[freesound.org](https://freesound.org) under CC0 (public domain), as each mod's `sources/sounds.toml` lists. They stay
free for anyone to use.
