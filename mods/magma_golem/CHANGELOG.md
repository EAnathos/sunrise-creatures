# Changelog

Each version's section says what changed in it (`cargo creature release` needs one before it tags the version).

## 0.1.0

- First version, in development: the magma golem, six blocks tall, drawn with its own model and animated, hit where
  its arms are too, its seams
  full-bright and its light; asleep as a mound in the Nether's basalt deltas and wastes near lava, waking when a
  player comes near, hits it or mines by it; a mini-boss with a boss bar that runs at you: the fist slam, the ground
  pound, the leap, the magma glob (held overhead in its fist, thrown fast and nearly flat, a small blast and fire where it
  lands), healing in lava and fire, enraged under half health; cooled by snowballs and splash water bottles; its spawn egg, its
  page in the creature guide and its four facts; its drops: its magma heart, magma cream and blocks, 50 experience;
  the molten pickaxe (a netherite pickaxe and a magma heart in a smithing table), which smelts what it mines.
