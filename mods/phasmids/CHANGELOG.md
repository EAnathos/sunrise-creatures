# Changelog

Each version's section is its registry changelog (`cargo creature release`).

## 0.1.0

- First release on Sunrise: stick insects (oak, birch, spruce, jungle) and leaf insects (green, dry) on the leaves of
  their trees, still by day and crawling over the canopy at night; spot them by looking at them a moment, counted on
  your guide; hit, they drop and play dead, then climb back up their tree; caught in the glass jar, they settle on a
  tree.
