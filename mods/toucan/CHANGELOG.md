# Changelog

Each version's section says what changed in it (`cargo creature release` needs one before it tags the version).

## 0.1.0

- First version: the toucan, out by day on the leaves of the jungles, hopping about the branches, resting, then
  flitting to the next tree, fleeing players and monsters, drawn with its own model and calling with its own croaks.
  Tamed with a thrown melon slice, it follows its friend or sits and stays. Its drops (feathers, raw toucan, cooked
  three ways), spawn egg and guide page.
