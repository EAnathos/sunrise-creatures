# Changelog

Each version's section says what changed in it (`cargo creature release` needs one before it tags the version).

## 0.1.0

- First version, in development: the elephant, roaming the savannas in small herds, drawn with its own model and
  animated; it charges whoever hits it; its own stats; tamed with thrown sugar cane, it follows its owner or
  stays, and a hay bale heals it; saddled and dyed, it carries its owner, who steers, and a second rider; two
  chests a flank, 54 slots a side, in its owner's screen. Its own sounds. Its spawn egg and its page in the creature guide.
