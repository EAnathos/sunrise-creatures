# Changelog

Each version's section says what changed in it (`cargo creature release` needs one before it tags the version).

## 0.1.0

- First version: the elephant, roaming the savannas in small herds, drawn with its own models (baby, juvenile, adult)
  and animated, charging whoever hits it or its riders, fleeing mice. Tamed with thrown sugar cane, it follows its
  owner or stays; a hay bale heals it, makes a young one grow and puts a grown one in love to breed. Saddled and dyed,
  it carries its owner, who steers, and a second rider; two chests a flank, 54 slots a side, in its owner's screen.
  Its own stats, sounds, spawn egg and guide page; its meat, its ivory tusk and the ceremonial trumpet.
