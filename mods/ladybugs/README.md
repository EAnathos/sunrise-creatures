# Ladybugs

![](../../docs/models/ladybugs-ladybug-front.png)

A mod of the [Sunrise Creatures](../../README.md) series, **planned**: ladybugs crawling on the flowers and leaves of
meadows and forests, red, yellow, orange or black, gathering in clusters on cold nights. One that lands on you brings
luck, for as many minutes as it has spots: count them, and look for the rare kinds. Catch one in a glass jar and it
settles on your flowers. **Requires** [Sunrise Creatures](https://playsunrise.net/mods/creature), the series' library.

Not playable yet: the design is in [SPECS.md](SPECS.md).
