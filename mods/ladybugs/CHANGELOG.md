# Changelog

Each version's section is its registry changelog (`cargo creature release`).

## 0.1.0

- First release on Sunrise: ladybugs on the flowers of meadows, forests and swamps in five kinds (seven-spot,
  two-spot, twenty-two-spot, orange, harlequin), flying short hops between flowers, crawling and climbing about,
  gathering at night and in the cold, sheltering under leaves in the rain; one that lands on a player standing still
  gives Luck for as many minutes as it has spots; caught in the glass jar, it settles on a flower patch; chickens peck
  them up; the "All the spots" fact.
